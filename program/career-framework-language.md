# Career Framework Language
 
Suggested language for integrating Security Champion participation into your organization's engineering career framework. Adapt to match your existing level definitions and language conventions.
 
Work with HR and engineering leadership to determine where this fits. The goal is for champion participation to be visible, attributed, and valued in performance conversations — not treated as extracurricular.
 
---
 
## Suggested Framework Language by Level
 
### Mid-Level Engineer (Yellow / Green Belt Alignment)
 
**Security and Quality**
- Actively participates in security practices for their team's codebase, including code review, dependency health, and scanner triage
- Documents recurring security patterns as reusable guidance for the team
- Demonstrates awareness of OWASP Top 10 risks and applies secure coding practices in daily work
*Security Champions context: Yellow and Green belt participation directly evidences these criteria.*
 
---
 
### Senior Engineer (Green / Blue Belt Alignment)
 
**Security and Quality**
- Serves as a security resource for their team — triages findings, reviews PRs for security issues, and participates in threat modeling for features in their area
- Creates and maintains security-focused SOPs and patterns used by peers
- Proactively identifies security risks in design and implementation decisions
**Influence and Mentorship**
- Shares security knowledge with teammates through code review, documentation, and informal guidance
- Helps newer engineers navigate security tooling and scanner outputs
*Security Champions context: Green and Blue belt participation directly evidences both sets of criteria.*
 
---
 
### Staff Engineer (Blue / Brown Belt Alignment)
 
**Technical Leadership**
- Designs and promotes secure patterns and reference implementations adopted across multiple teams
- Leads threat modeling for high-risk features or services in their domain
- Advises on security architecture decisions and trade-offs at the service or platform level
**Organizational Impact**
- Mentors engineers across teams on security topics, multiplying security capability beyond their immediate team
- Contributes to security process improvements that reduce friction for the engineering organization
*Security Champions context: Blue and Brown belt participation, especially pod lead responsibilities, directly evidences Staff-level criteria.*
 
---
 
### Principal Engineer (Brown / Black Belt Alignment)
 
**Strategic Technical Contribution**
- Defines security standards and golden paths adopted across the engineering organization
- Co-owns a security domain alongside the security team, serving as the engineering-side expert and decision-maker
- Shapes tooling, automation, and process strategy at the organizational level
**Engineering Culture**
- Actively develops the next generation of security-capable engineers through mentorship, workshops, and program participation
- Represents engineering security capability at executive and cross-functional forums
*Security Champions context: Brown and Black belt participation, particularly specialization track ownership and executive presentation, directly evidences Principal-level criteria.*
 
---
 
## Performance Review Language Examples
 
Use these in self-reviews or manager reviews to make champion participation visible and specific.
 
**Yellow/Green Belt:**
> "As a Yellow belt Security Champion, I triaged [X] SAST and SCA findings this quarter, resolving [Y] before they reached production. I created [Z] SOPs documenting recurring patterns now used by the team. This work directly reduced the security review burden on the central security team and improved our team's PR quality."
 
**Green/Blue Belt:**
> "As a Green belt Security Champion, I served as the first-line security contact for [team/service area]. I led threat model participation for [feature name], identified [X] risks that were mitigated before the feature shipped, and reduced our team's MTTR on medium-severity findings by [Y]%. I also provided security code review feedback on [X] PRs this quarter."
 
**Blue/Brown Belt:**
> "As a Blue belt Security Champion and pod lead, I mentored [X] Yellow and Green belt champions through their first triage assignments. I led [X] threat model sessions independently and co-owned triage for the [product area] scanner queue. I contributed to the redesign of our triage workflow, reducing average time-to-triage from [X] to [Y] days."
 
**Brown/Black Belt:**
> "As a Senior Champion, I co-designed the [specific pattern or reference architecture] now used across [X] teams. I delivered an internal workshop on [topic] attended by [X] engineers. My work in the [specialization area] has been directly referenced in [QBR / CISO briefing / compliance review], and I have mentored [X] champions through their Green-to-Blue progression this year."
