## Communication Calendar Template
 
A suggested cadence for program communications across a full year. Adjust based on your organization's communication culture and meeting saturation.
 
---
 
### Weekly (Security Team)
 
| Communication | Channel | Owner | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Champion shout-outs | Champion channel | Security team | Specific, named contributions from the past week |
| Triage assignment notifications | Champion channel or DM | Security team | New findings assigned with context |
| Unblocking and guidance responses | Champion channel | Security team | Respond to champion questions within 1 business day |
 
---
 
### Monthly
 
| Communication | Channel | Owner | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly meetup | Video call | Security team | See agenda template above |
| Manager summary | Email or DM to managers | Security team | 3–5 bullet summary of their champion's contributions and health indicators |
| Program health dashboard update | Champion channel | Security team | Key metrics snapshot — keep it visual and brief |
 
---
 
### Quarterly
 
| Communication | Channel | Owner | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Champion Spotlight newsletter | Engineering-wide | Security team | Feature 2–4 champions with named contributions; metrics summary; upcoming events |
| Metrics awards | Champion channel + manager notification | Security team | Award categories: highest vuln reduction, best PR reviewer, top SOP contributor, most improved |
| Belt promotion announcements | Champion channel + manager email | Security team | Announce every promotion publicly with specific rationale |
| Director and VP program summary | Executive report or meeting | Security team + executive sponsor | Business-outcome framing: findings prevented, MTTR improvement, workload reduction |
| Training deadline reminders | Champion channel | Security team | 2-week and 1-week reminders before quarterly training completions |

### Annual
 
| Communication | Channel | Owner | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|

---
 
### Communication Principles
 
**Specificity over frequency.** One specific, meaningful shout-out per week is worth more than daily generic encouragement.
 
**Manager-facing communications should use business language.** Vulnerability counts and CVSS scores mean little to a people manager. "Your champion helped prevent three security issues from reaching production this month" means a lot.
 
**Executive communications should connect to business outcomes.** Delivery risk reduction, MTTR improvement, rework avoided. Not security jargon.
 
**Do not let the channel go silent.** A champion channel with no activity for two weeks signals the program is not active. Even a short "interesting finding of the week" post maintains energy between major communications.
