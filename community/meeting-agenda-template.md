## Monthly Champion Meetup Agenda Template
 
**Recommended duration:** 60–90 minutes
**Cadence:** Monthly
**Required attendance:** Yellow and Green belts. Blue+ encouraged.
**Format:** Video call with optional in-person component for co-located teams.
 
---
 
### Pre-Meetup Checklist (Security Team, 48 hours before)
 
- [ ] Agenda posted in champion channel
- [ ] Any pre-reading or materials linked in the agenda post
- [ ] Guest speaker confirmed (if applicable)
- [ ] Recognition items prepared (shout-outs, milestone acknowledgments)
- [ ] Technical topic or demo ready
---
 
### Agenda
 
**[0:00 – 0:05] Welcome and Housekeeping**
 
- Welcome any new champions joining for the first time
- Quick reminder of the meeting norms (camera encouraged, questions in chat or unmuted)
- Note any agenda changes
---
 
**[0:05 – 0:15] Recognition and Wins**
 
This section is the most important 10 minutes of the meetup. Do not skip it or rush it.
 
- Shout out specific champions for contributions from the past month
  - Name the person, name the contribution, name why it mattered
  - "Alex triaged 14 findings this month and caught a medium-severity injection issue at PR stage that would have reached production" lands. "Great work everyone" does not.
- Acknowledge any belt promotions since the last meetup
- Acknowledge any milestones (90-day probation completions, first SOPs published, etc.)
- Open it up: "Any wins you want to share from your team this month?"
---
 
**[0:15 – 0:40] Technical Topic or Guest Session**
 
Options (rotate to keep meetups varied):
 
| Format | Description | When to Use |
|---|---|---|
| Security team presentation | 15–20 minute walkthrough of a topic relevant to champions | Introducing new tools, processes, or vulnerability classes |
| Champion-led demo | A champion presents something they learned or built | Phase 2+ when champions have enough experience to teach |
| Guest speaker | External or internal speaker on a relevant topic | When an expert is available; adds credibility and variety |
| Hands-on lab | Short collaborative triage or threat modeling exercise | Phase 2+; keeps meetups practical, not just passive |
| CTF preview or debrief | Preview upcoming competition or debrief a past one | Before and after CTF events |
| Open Q&A with Security team | Champions submit questions in advance; Security team answers live | When champions have accumulated questions; builds transparency |
 
**Topic selection guidance:**
- Rotate between defensive (how to find and fix) and contextual (why this matters, real-world examples)
- Bias toward topics champions can apply in their next sprint, not theoretical content
- AI and emerging threat topics should appear at least quarterly given the pace of change
---
 
**[0:40 – 0:55] Program Updates and Discussion**
 
- Metrics update: brief summary of program health indicators (keep this concise — one slide or 2–3 data points)
- Upcoming events: CTFs, training deadlines, conference sponsorship opportunities
- Process changes: any updates to playbooks, scope, tooling, or belt criteria
- Open discussion: "What is the most annoying security friction your team dealt with this month?"
This last question is one of the most valuable things you can ask regularly. The answers tell you where the program needs to improve, where the Security team needs to improve, and where engineering tooling needs to improve.
 
---
 
**[0:55 – 1:00] Wrap-Up**
 
- Recap of action items from discussion
- Next meetup date and any advance topics
- Informal chat time for anyone who wants to stay on
---
 
### Post-Meetup (Security Team, within 48 hours)
 
- [ ] Meeting notes posted in champion channel (action items, key decisions, recognition recap)
- [ ] Any follow-up items assigned and tracked
- [ ] Attendance recorded for belt progress tracking
- [ ] Shout-out content saved for quarterly spotlight
